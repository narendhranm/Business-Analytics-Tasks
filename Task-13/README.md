# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset using Tableau Public to identify business insights, sales trends, product performance, regional profitability, and customer purchasing patterns.

## Project Links

**Tableau Public Dashboard:**  
(https://public.tableau.com/views/TAsk-13/Dashboard1?:language=en-GB&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)


## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Understand customer segment performance.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## Dataset
**Dataset Name:** Sample Superstore

**Data Fields:**
- Order Date
- Ship Date
- Customer Name
- Segment
- Region
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Product Name
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Discount

## Tools & Technologies
- Tableau Public
- Sample Superstore Dataset

## Dashboard Visualizations

1. Monthly Sales Trend
2. Sales by Category
3. Profit by Region
4. Top 10 Products
5. Sales by Customer Segment
6. Discount vs Profit

## Key Findings
- Technology generates the highest sales among the three categories.
- West is the most profitable region.
- Central is the least profitable region.
- Consumer customers contribute the highest sales.
- Product-level analysis identifies top-performing products.
- Discount analysis helps understand potential profitability issues.

## Conclusion
The analysis provides an overview of business performance across different categories, regions, products, and customer segments. It demonstrates how data visualization can support better business decisions.

## Suggestions
- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Improve marketing in low-performing regions.
- Develop targeted promotions for Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Monitor discounts to protect profit margins.
- Improve inventory planning based on product demand.

## Recommendations
- Invest in profitable product categories.
- Develop regional marketing strategies.
- Monitor monthly sales and profit.
- Review pricing and discount strategies.
- Regularly analyze customer purchasing patterns.

## Project Outcome
Successfully developed an interactive Tableau Public dashboard that transforms sales data into meaningful business insights.
