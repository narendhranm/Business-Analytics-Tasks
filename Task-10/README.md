# Business Performance Analysis Using Heat Map – Tableau Public

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze business performance across different product categories and identify high-performing and low-performing areas using a **Heat Map** in Tableau Public. Additional visualizations and interactive filters are used to understand sales patterns and business performance.

---

## Problem Statement

A business dataset containing multiple categorical variables and numerical measures was selected from Kaggle. The dataset was analyzed using Tableau Public to create a Heat Map and additional visualizations.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

* Highest-performing category combinations
* Lowest-performing category combinations
* Categories with consistently high or low sales
* Sales trends and patterns
* Business insights and recommendations

---

## Dataset

**Dataset Used:** Superstore Sales Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset contains information about customer orders, products, categories, regions, sales, and other business-related attributes.

### Important Fields Used

| Field        | Type        | Purpose                      |
| ------------ | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| Category     | Categorical | Main product category        |
| Sub-Category | Categorical | Product sub-category         |
| Sales        | Numerical   | Business performance measure |
| Segment      | Categorical | Customer segment             |
| Region       | Categorical | Geographic region            |
| Order Date   | Date        | Sales trend analysis         |

---

## Tools Used

* Tableau Public
* Kaggle
* Microsoft Excel / CSV

---

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Heat Map – Category vs Sub-Category

The Heat Map compares **Category** and **Sub-Category** using **Sales** as the numerical measure.

* Rows: Category
* Columns: Sub-Category
* Color: Sales
* Label: Sales

Color intensity represents sales performance. Darker cells represent higher sales values, while lighter cells represent lower sales values.

---

### 2. Sales by Category – Bar Chart

A bar chart was created to compare total sales across different product categories.

**Fields Used:**

* Category
* Sales

This visualization helps identify which major product categories contribute more to overall sales.

---

### 3. Sales Trend – Line Chart

A line chart was created to analyze changes in sales over time.

**Fields Used:**

* Order Date
* Sales

This visualization helps identify increasing, decreasing, and fluctuating sales patterns.

---

### 4. Sales by Segment – Pie Chart

A pie chart was created to analyze the contribution of different customer segments.

**Fields Used:**

* Segment
* Sales

The visualization compares sales generated by Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

---

## Interactive Filter

A **Region** filter was added to the dashboard.

The filter allows users to select different regions and dynamically analyze the corresponding:

* Heat Map
* Category Sales
* Sales Trend
* Segment Sales

This makes the dashboard interactive and allows users to explore regional performance.

---

## Heat Map Analysis

### Highest-Performing Combination

The highest-performing combination is identified by locating the cell with the highest Sales value in the Heat Map.

**Observation:**

The category and sub-category combination with the highest sales represents the strongest-performing product group in the dataset.

---

### Lowest-Performing Combination

The lowest-performing combination is identified by locating the cell with the lowest Sales value.

**Observation:**

The category and sub-category combination with the lowest sales represents an area that may require further business analysis.

---

### Consistently High-Performing Categories

Categories containing multiple high-value sub-categories show relatively consistent sales performance.

The Heat Map helps identify these categories through groups of darker cells.

---

### Consistently Low-Performing Categories

Categories containing multiple low-value sub-categories show relatively weaker sales performance.

These areas can be examined further to understand customer demand and product performance.

---

## Noticeable Patterns

The analysis of the Heat Map and additional visualizations provides the following patterns:

1. Sales performance differs significantly between product categories and sub-categories.
2. Some sub-categories contribute considerably more sales than others.
3. Sales contribution varies across customer segments.
4. Sales change over time, with periods of relatively higher and lower performance.
5. Regional filtering allows differences in business performance between regions to be identified.

---

## 5 Business Insights

1. **Category Performance:** Sales are not evenly distributed across all product categories and sub-categories. Some product groups contribute significantly more to total sales.

2. **High-Performing Products:** The highest-value cells in the Heat Map indicate product combinations with strong customer demand and sales performance.

3. **Low-Performing Products:** Low-value cells identify product combinations that may require additional investigation regarding demand, pricing, marketing, or inventory.

4. **Customer Segments:** Different customer segments contribute different amounts of sales, providing useful information about the customer groups generating revenue.

5. **Regional Performance:** The interactive Region filter makes it possible to compare business performance across different geographic regions.

---

## 2 Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

The business should focus inventory planning and marketing efforts on high-performing category and sub-category combinations to maintain product availability and support continued sales.

### Recommendation 2

Low-performing product combinations should be investigated further. The business can evaluate pricing, customer demand, marketing strategies, and inventory levels to identify possible opportunities for improvement.

## Dashboard

The final Tableau dashboard combines all four visualizations:

* Heat Map
* Sales by Category Bar Chart
* Sales Trend Line Chart
* Sales by Segment Pie Chart

An interactive **Region filter** is also included to allow users to explore the data dynamically.

---

## Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance across product categories, sub-categories, customer segments, and regions. The Heat Map makes it easy to identify high- and low-performing product combinations, while the additional visualizations provide information about category performance, sales trends, and customer segments.

This analysis can help businesses understand sales patterns and identify areas that require further attention.
