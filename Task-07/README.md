# 📊 Business Performance Analysis Using Tableau

Link : https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/naren.m6170/viz/BALWEEK7_17884179580580/Dashboard1#1

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes business performance using **Tableau Public** and the **Sample Superstore dataset**. The main objective is to identify relationships between sales and profit, analyze trends over time, and discover business insights that can support better decision-making.

The project includes a **Scatter Plot** as the primary visualization along with three additional visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze business performance using a **Scatter Plot in Tableau** and identify important trends, relationships, and changes that can support business decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore

The dataset contains business-related information including:

* Order Date
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Discount
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Region
* Customer Information
* Product Information

**Time-related field:** Order Date

**Main numerical measures:** Sales and Profit

---

## 📊 Visualizations Created

### 1. Scatter Plot – Sales vs Profit

The main visualization analyzes the relationship between **Sales and Profit**.

* X-axis: Sales
* Y-axis: Profit
* Color: Category
* Detail: Order ID / Sub-Category
* Trend Line: Linear

This visualization helps identify profitable orders, low-profit orders, and loss-making orders.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart is used to analyze how sales change over time.

**Fields used:**

* Order Date
* Sales

This helps identify increases, decreases, peaks, and fluctuations in sales.

### 3. Sales by Category

A bar chart compares sales performance across different product categories.

**Fields used:**

* Category
* Sales

This helps identify which categories contribute more to overall sales.

### 4. Profit by Region

A bar chart compares profitability across different regions.

**Fields used:**

* Region
* Profit

This helps identify regions with stronger and weaker profitability.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

The Scatter Plot helps demonstrate the relationship between Sales and Profit. In general, higher sales tend to be associated with higher profits, but some high-sales orders may still generate relatively low or negative profit.

The analysis also shows that business performance varies across product categories and regions. The sales trend over time helps identify periods of stronger and weaker performance.

These findings indicate that **sales volume alone should not be used to measure business success**. Profitability, product category, region, discounts, and changes over time should also be considered.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

* Focus on products and categories generating higher profits.
* Investigate orders with high sales but low or negative profit.
* Review discount and pricing strategies.
* Identify high-performing regions and strengthen their strategies.
* Analyze low-performing regions to understand the causes.
* Use historical sales trends for better planning and forecasting.
